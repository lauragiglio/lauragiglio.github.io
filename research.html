Research

Here is a summary of my current and previous research projects.

Cross-decoding of word production and comprehension in post-stroke aphasia

Thanks to an SNSF Postdoctoral Fellowship I was recently awarded, I am setting up an experiment to investigate the neural correlates of semantic and phonological processing across word production and comprehension using EEG. In this project, I aim to take advantage of multivariate pattern analysis to perform temporal decoding across production and comprehension in neurotypical speakers and speakers with aphasia.
